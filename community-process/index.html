How people join, contribute, and move through CIBC

A short orientation for members who could not attend the 2026-05-04 town hall: who shows up, what each person can reach, and how someone moves from a curious stranger to a contributor.

Draft 2026-06-18 · Source: 2026-05-04 town hall · Proposed, gates not yet locked

The people CIBC is built around

Four roles are stages on one path as trust grows. Two sit outside it.

Aware The Curious Citizen Found CIBC through a link, a post, or word of mouth. Does not know us yet. "Show me what this is before I commit to anything." Linktree
Interested The Watcher Wants a closer look. Willing to lurk and learn before raising a hand. "Let me put a toe in the water and see what's going on." Broadcasting Public wiki
New member Aspiring builder Crossed the membrane. Aligned, introduced, and inside the group. "Help me get oriented and find my people fast." Full Telegram
Contributor Builder Put work in: a project, a curation, a build, or showing up to run things. "Let me add my work and gain visibility for it." GitHub
Enabler The Steward One of the rotating few who welcome, vet, and keep the commons coherent. No single "community manager." "Keep the membrane real without making joining hard."
Adjacent The External Community Another group that wants what CIBC has. Not a member: a community CIBC helps set up. "Stand up our own stack so we can run this ourselves."

Two roles sit outside the ladder. The steward tends every gate (welcoming, vetting, keeping the commons coherent). The external community walks a parallel path: CIBC helps it stand up its own stack and then hands it over, with no lock-in.

Method note

For: CIBC members who could not attend the 2026-05-04 town hall, so they can see the membership model the call sketched out without listening to a two-hour recording.

Source: everything here is drawn from that town hall transcript and the captured ideas and jobs-to-be-done outline that followed it. The channel-to-tier mapping (Linktree, broadcasting, full Telegram, GitHub) is referenced in pieces across the call and assembled here into one model for the first time.

Status: proposed, not decided. The walls and gates are a working design, not policy. Open questions the call left open: whether Harmonica is the right intake tool, how wiki edits are governed, and whether the club charges anything. The two-week contribution window and the roughly-three-meetups trust step are the call's own heuristics, not measured figures. Confirm the gates with the stewards before treating any of this as settled.
