Standing rhythm: Friday calls until the first demo day

Weekly Friday call, one hour, focused decisions only. The first cycle (May into mid-June) is dense because the first demo day is being built from scratch. If a call is light, end early. The point is to keep the threads from going stale, not to fill the hour.

Each Friday answers three questions: what shipped this week, what's stuck, what does next week need. Recap goes into the planning log as a frame and a downloadable transcript. Anyone who can't make it reads the recap.
