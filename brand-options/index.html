Citizen Infrastructure Builders Club

Brand Option A

This direction pairs Art Nouveau with Bauhaus because CIBC needs a visual language that feels civic, durable, and hand-built without losing structure. Art Nouveau gives it care and ornament. Bauhaus gives it the grid, repeatability, and system logic an agent can reuse.

03 · Graphics

Connected Plane motif

Graphics sit after text and color so the visual system is read as an application of the foundation, not a separate mood board.

Core motif

Square isometric plane

The plane stays visible. Art Nouveau ornament frames the structure, while Bauhaus geometry carries the paths, city blocks, and civic infrastructure.

Asset families
  • Hero scene with text-safe area.
  • Dynamic cityscape from supplied reference photos.
  • Member character or work scene from uploaded image.
  • Small icons, connector pattern, and scene props.
Generation rule

Cutouts and icons are generated one at a time on chroma-key green #00ff00, then recovered as durable files before placement.
